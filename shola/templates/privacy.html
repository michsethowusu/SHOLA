Privacy

What we hold, and what we publish

SHOLA collects as little as it can. There are no trackers, no analytics and no advertising on this site.

What we ask you for

  • Your email — to send you words, and to let you back in. There is no password; the link in your email is how you log in.
  • Your name — shown on the leaderboard beside the number of items you have checked, unless you turn that off.
  • Your language, and which days and what time of day suit you — so words arrive when you can answer them.
  • A photo, if you choose to add one. Optional, and shown in one place only: the leaderboard.

What you give us by answering

Each answer records the item, the language, what you chose or typed, and when. That is the point of the project — it is the data everyone is here to build.

What is published

The translations and how many speakers agreed with each one, through an open API and as a public dataset, under CC BY-NC 4.0.

The published data carries no names, no email addresses and no identifiers of any kind. There is no field in it that points back to a person. The only page where a contributor is named is the leaderboard, and you can keep yourself off it from your settings, at any time, in either direction.

What we never do

  • No analytics, no tracking pixels, no advertising.
  • We do not sell your details, or share them for marketing.
  • One cookie, which remembers your link so the menu can offer a way back to your words. Nothing else is stored in your browser.

Who else handles it

  • Brevo delivers our email, so it processes your address.
  • Cloudflare sits in front of the site and sees the IP addresses of visitors, as any website host does.
  • Hugging Face hosts the machine-translation files. Those are machine output and contain nothing about you.

How long we keep it

Until you delete your account. The database is backed up nightly to off-site storage, so a copy may persist in backups for up to sixty days after you go.

What you can do

  • Change your days, your time of day, or pause — from your settings.
  • Keep your name off the leaderboard, and put it back later.
  • Stop the emails without deleting anything.
  • Delete your account. Your name and email are removed from our records for good.

Deleting keeps the translations you confirmed. An item you helped settle was settled, other speakers' answers were counted against yours, and withdrawing them would change results that are not yours alone. Those answers were never published under your name, so there is no name in them to remove.
