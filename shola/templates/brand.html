What to say

  • It is two minutes. People assume volunteering means hours.
  • No app, no password. A link arrives by email and opens straight into the words.
  • The result is free for anyone to build on. That is how a language earns its place in the apps, services and businesses Ghanaians use every day.

Please do not

  • Say it pays. It is volunteer work.
  • Promise a language that is not open. Twi, Ewe, Ga and Dagbani are collecting today; for the rest, say they can join the list.
  • Recolour or stretch the logo, or set the name in another typeface.
