Tiers

Words are worked in bands of commonness. Nothing in a tier is handed out until the tier above it is closed, so the words people use most are settled first.

A word is settled when two speakers choose the same wording. It is contested when five verdicts arrive without two agreeing — several wordings can be equally right, so the word is closed, every variant kept, and the tier moves on.
